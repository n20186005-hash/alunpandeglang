# Local visual assets

The five WebP illustrations in `public/generated/` were supplied as project-made decorative artwork: the guide mark, paper texture, botanical corner, compass ribbon and abstract morning light. They are illustrations, not documentary evidence of the square or official emblems.

Earlier uncredited location photographs have been removed. No historic image of a different square is substituted for the present place. All active image paths are local and reproducible from `public/`.
